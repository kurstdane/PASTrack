Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about tracking and submissions.

How do I track my property tax declaration?
Enter your Tracking ID in the search bar at the top of the tracking page. The system will retrieve the current status and location of your documents instantly.
Where can I find my Tracking ID?
Your Tracking ID is printed on the Acknowledgment Receipt provided by the Municipal Assessor's Office when you submitted your documents.
What should I do if my Tracking ID shows "Not Found"?
Please ensure you typed the ID exactly as printed. If it still does not appear, the Municipal Assessor may not have logged it into the PASTrack system yet. Please contact your local assessor's office for clarification.
How long does it take for a tax declaration to be approved?
Processing typically takes 5 to 10 working days upon receipt of complete documents by the Provincial Assessor's Office, depending on the complexity of the transaction.
What are the common reasons for a returned or rejected application?
Applications are often returned due to lacking documentary requirements (such as the eCAR from BIR or updated tax receipts), missing signatures, or discrepancies in the technical description of the property.
Can I submit my documents directly to the Provincial Capitol?
No, all submissions must originate from the Municipal or City Assessor's Office where the property is located. They will conduct the initial processing before forwarding the documents to the Province via PASTrack.
How will I know if my tax declaration is ready for pickup?
Once the status on PASTrack shows "Approved and Released," your documents have been forwarded back to your local Municipal/City Assessor's Office, where you can claim your owner's copy.
Do I need to pay any fees when tracking my documents online?
No, the PASTrack online portal is a completely free service provided by the Province of Cebu. Standard processing fees only apply at your local assessor's office during the actual submission.
What is an eCAR and why is it required?
An Electronic Certificate Authorizing Registration (eCAR) from the Bureau of Internal Revenue proves that necessary taxes (like Capital Gains or Estate Tax) have been paid. It is a mandatory requirement for transferring property ownership.
Who can I contact if I have further questions?
You can use the "Contact Support" button below to send us a direct message, or you can reach out to your Municipal Assessor. You may also email the Provincial Assessor at assessor@cebu.gov.ph.
