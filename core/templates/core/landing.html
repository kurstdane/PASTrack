The PASTrack Journey

A transparent, step-by-step view of how your property documents are processed between the Municipal Assessor and the Provincial Capitol.

01

Document Intake

Your local Municipal Assessor receives and logs your documents into the PASTrack ecosystem, generating your unique Tracking ID.

02

Examiner Review

Provincial examiners conduct a rigorous verification of your submitted documents to ensure compliance with assessment protocols.

03

Tax Mapping

Property boundaries are verified against digital and physical tax maps to guarantee spatial and geographic accuracy.

04

Provincial Approval

The documents undergo final review and receive the formal signature of the Provincial Assessor for legal validation.

05

Final Numbering

An official Tax Declaration number is formally assigned, officially recording the property in the provincial registry.

06

Ready for Release

The finalized documents are dispatched back to your Municipal Assessor's office, ready for your collection.

Why PASTrack?

A secure platform designed to serve both the citizens of Cebu and the dedicated staff of our local government units.

Legal Security

Ensures every transaction is strictly monitored and recorded, safeguarding your legal property rights.

Accelerated Processing

Replaces manual logbooks with instant digital transfers, drastically reducing typical assessment turnaround times.

Unified Network

Connects 50 municipalities and component cities seamlessly with the central Provincial Capitol database.

Document Requirements

To avoid delays in processing, please ensure you have secured the following basic requirements before filing your declaration at your Municipal Assessor's Office. Certain transactions may require additional supporting documents.

  • Verified Secretary Certificate (if Corporation)
  • Certified True Copy of Property Title
  • Latest Tax Receipt or Tax Clearance
  • Affidavit of Ownership (if untitled property)
  • Valid Government-Issued Identification

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my property tax declaration?
Enter your Tracking ID in the search bar at the top of this page. The system will retrieve the current status and location of your documents instantly.
Where can I find my Tracking ID?
Your Tracking ID is printed on the Acknowledgment Receipt provided by the Municipal Assessor's Office when you submitted your documents.
What should I do if my Tracking ID shows "Not Found"?
Please ensure you typed the ID exactly as printed. If it still does not appear, the Municipal Assessor may not have logged it into the PASTrack system yet. Please contact your local assessor's office for clarification.
